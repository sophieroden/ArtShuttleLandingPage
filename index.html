Stop Risking Your Final-Year Degree Project on Unreliable Dublin Commuter Transport

A dedicated micro-courier shuttle service for commuting art, architecture, and design students transporting fragile physical models and canvases from suburban family homes directly to campus studio gates.

Try the Interactive Demo ➔

1. The Problem & Who Has It

Customer Segment: Final-year art, design, and architecture students (User) commuting daily from suburban family homes into Dublin colleges (e.g., NCAD, TU Dublin Grangegorman, IADT) who rely on erratic public transit. The buyer is the student or supporting parent funding their materials.

"My bus got cancelled in the freezing rain, my large architectural model warped on the crowded replacement bus, and I've wasted €80 in materials right before my final submission." (Invented quote)[cite: 3]

Specific Requirements

  • Secure van transit designed specifically for oversized canvases, framed art, and 3D scale models.
  • Real-time route tracking synchronized with Dublin public transit delay patterns.
  • Guaranteed morning pickup windows from outer Dublin commuter hubs (Swords, Maynooth, Bray, Lucan).
  • Protective weather-sealed transit crates to prevent moisture and impact damage.
  • Direct drop-off at college studio loading bays before morning critique deadlines.

How They Cope Today & Existing Alternatives

Students currently scramble to wrap projects in black bin bags while begging family members to drive them through M50 traffic. Existing alternatives checked at source:

  • An Post Express Post / Parcel Delivery: Checked 24 October 2026 at anpost.com. Limitation: Does not handle oversized delicate 3D models or offer fragile studio drop-off inside campus buildings.
  • Free Now / Uber Taxi Courier: Checked 24 October 2026 at free-now.com. Limitation: Drivers frequently refuse oversized or fragile student art boards due to lack of interior secure racking.

2. The Solution Fits the Requirements

What it is: ArtShuttle Dublin is a mobile-web scheduling portal connecting student commuters with dedicated micro-van couriers equipped with specialized padding for fragile academic work.

How It Works in 3 Steps

  1. Book Route: Select your commuter origin station and campus destination on your phone.
  2. Secure Crate: Choose weather-sealed crate protection for fragile glass or foam board models.
  3. Studio Drop-Off: Meet the shuttle van at your suburban hub and track it directly to your college gate.

Priced at an assumed base fare of €10.00 to €14.00 per transit run depending on distance, plus a €2.50 crate rental fee. Revenue is split 75% to independent verified van drivers and 25% platform commission to sustain server and dispatch operations[cite: 3].
